# Sales Data Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

## VedGrow Internship | Data Science Task 2

### Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail sales dataset to uncover revenue trends, top-performing products, regional performance, and year-over-year growth. Using Python, Pandas, and Plotly, an interactive dashboard was created to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

### Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales performance, identify key revenue drivers, and create an interactive dashboard that supports data-driven business decision-making.

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Plotly
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** Retail Sales Dataset (Superstore)
- **Records:** Approximately 10,000 sales transactions
- **Columns:** 21 business-related attributes
- **Key Fields:** Order Date, Category, Product Name, Region, Sales, Profit, Customer Segment

---

## Analysis Performed

- Data loading and cleaning
- Missing value inspection
- Duplicate record removal
- Date conversion for time-series analysis
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Category-wise revenue analysis
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Top-performing regions
- Year-over-Year (YoY) revenue growth calculation
- Interactive sales dashboard using Plotly

---

## Key Insights

### **Insight 1: Technology Generated the Highest Revenue**

The Technology category generated the highest overall revenue, making it the strongest-performing product category.

### **Insight 2: Revenue Showed Consistent Growth Over Time**

Yearly revenue displayed an overall upward trend, indicating steady business growth across the available years.

### **Insight 3: A Small Number of Products Drove Significant Revenue**

The top-performing products contributed a substantial share of total revenue, highlighting the importance of key products.

### **Insight 4: Regional Performance Varied**

Revenue differed across regions, showing that some markets consistently outperformed others.

### **Insight 5: Revenue Was Unevenly Distributed**

Most sales were relatively small while a few high-value transactions contributed significantly to total revenue.
